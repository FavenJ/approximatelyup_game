**The two keys that define Approximately Up are G and R: G toggles garage edit mode, and holding R starts (or ends) fly mode.** Everything else builds on those two modes. Press M in-game at any time to open the manual.

## Garage edit mode controls

Garage mode is where you place and connect parts freely. The confirmed shortcuts from the tutorial flow are:

- **G** — toggle garage edit mode on or off
- **Ctrl+G, then drag** — box-select multiple parts at once
- **C** — clone the selected component
- **X** — delete the selected parts
- **H** — show garage debug info when parts behave oddly
- **B** — blueprint a design, once that feature is unlocked

Box-select is the fix for a misaligned frame: select everything, then drag the whole ship into place instead of rebuilding.

## Fly mode controls

Hold **R** to enter fly mode — physics activate and gravity applies. Hold R again to end the flight, which resets the scene to its pre-flight state so crashes never cost you parts.

While flying, click a throttle, joystick, or button to focus it, then steer with **WASD or the mouse** depending on the control you selected. Press **Mouse 3 (middle click)** to snap a stuck lever or joystick back to neutral — do this before any panic rebuild.

## On foot and objectives

Double-tap **Left Shift** to toggle hover dampening while on foot. In ships, hover dampening is replaced by hover logic circuits you build yourself.

Press **O** to open the objectives screen, which lists missions by difficulty. Green missions teach single concepts; orange missions combine several systems at once.
