Approximately Up is built for online co-op with up to four players as much as solo engineering. Steam marketing literally sells the blame game: who forgot the cable, who mounted the thruster backward, who skipped the solar panels? This Multiplayer Hub collects co-op advice, role ideas, and links to deep guides so your crew launches together instead of arguing on the pad.

## How multiplayer works

Invite friends through the in-game invite player flow while hosting a session. All players share the same shipyard context: frames to weld, thrusters to orient, logic gates to miswire. Flight is first-person from the deck, so not everyone needs a pilot seat — engineers can stay in build mode while one player flies, or multiple monitors can expose radar to a navigator.

Co-op uses online interactivity and in-game chat per Steam feature tags. Voice chat is typically handled through Steam overlay or external Discord — many crews prefer Discord for push-to-talk while building.

## Why four players changes ship design

Solo ships optimize for one pair of hands. Co-op ships optimize for parallel tasks:

- <span className="text-base bg-gradient-to-r from-brand-theme to-brand-accent bg-clip-text text-transparent">One player wires RCS while another sets knobs on six thrusters.</span>
- <span className="text-base bg-gradient-to-r from-brand-theme to-brand-accent bg-clip-text text-transparent">A navigator watches radar and calls range while the pilot focuses on attitude.</span>
- <span className="text-base bg-gradient-to-r from-brand-theme to-brand-accent bg-clip-text text-transparent">A mission runner reads objective log entries so the crew does not launch without cargo.</span>
- <span className="text-base bg-gradient-to-r from-brand-theme to-brand-accent bg-clip-text text-transparent">Oversized cockpits with multiple monitor lines become worthwhile. Conversely, tiny racers become chaos when four avatars block cable runs.</span>

## Communication habits that prevent crashes

- <span className="text-base bg-gradient-to-r from-brand-theme to-brand-accent bg-clip-text text-transparent">Call arming</span><span className="text-base text-gray-300"> — "Entering fly mode in three" so nobody deletes a cable mid-throttle.</span>
- <span className="text-base bg-gradient-to-r from-brand-theme to-brand-accent bg-clip-text text-transparent">Confirm RCS colors</span><span className="text-base text-gray-300"> — green on the controller before anyone lifts off.</span>
- <span className="text-base bg-gradient-to-r from-brand-theme to-brand-accent bg-clip-text text-transparent">Read objectives aloud</span><span className="text-base text-gray-300"> — press O together before launch.</span>
- <span className="text-base bg-gradient-to-r from-brand-theme to-brand-accent bg-clip-text text-transparent">Name stations</span><span className="text-base text-gray-300"> — "We land at Earth North, not Moonstep."</span>
- <span className="text-base bg-gradient-to-r from-brand-theme to-brand-accent bg-clip-text text-transparent">Blame humor, then fix</span><span className="text-base text-gray-300"> — the game encourages jokes; follow with a Thrust calculator check.</span>

## Solo vs co-op progression

Mission unlocks and station permanence apply to the host's world state in standard co-op patterns — verify host rules in your session. Demo English-only UI affects international crews; full release languages help mixed-language groups — see Updates.

Parts unlocked through missions benefit the whole crew's build options when sharing a save. Treat unlock grinding as a group project rather than solo grind if everyone wants tier S thrusters.
