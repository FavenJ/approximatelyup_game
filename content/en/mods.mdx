**There is no confirmed mod support for Approximately Up yet.** We have not verified any official mod tools, Steam Workshop integration, or modding documentation, so we will not link you to downloads that may not be safe or real.

## What is not confirmed

Whether the developers plan official mod support is **to be confirmed**. Whether Steam Workshop will be enabled for the full release in August 2026 is also **to be confirmed**.

No community mod frameworks, loaders, or popular mods have been verified through official sources. If modding scenes form around the demo, they have not been officially acknowledged.

## A word of caution

Until official modding channels exist, treat any "Approximately Up mod" download with suspicion. Unverified executables for a new game are a classic malware vector.

This wiki will only ever link to mod resources once they are confirmed through official or clearly legitimate community channels.

## Where the community gathers instead

The confirmed community spaces are the Steam Community discussions for the game and Discord, where crews organize during demo periods. Finding co-op partners, sharing ship designs, and trading building advice all happen there today.

The in-game blueprint feature — pressing **B** once unlocked — is the confirmed built-in way to save and share ship designs. For many players, blueprint sharing covers much of what mods would offer.

## Stay updated

The full release in August 2026 brings more planets and co-op content, and official feature announcements will land on the Steam store page and Steam Community first. This page will be updated the moment mod support is officially confirmed or denied.
